# Branding & Identity

## Overview

- **Public Brand Name**: Forge
- **Internal Project Codename**: Operation Overthink

---

## Brand Persona & Tone

1. **Crafted & Focused**: Forge represents shaping raw knowledge into mastery through deliberate practice and collaboration.
2. **Community-Oriented**: Warm, encouraging, and transparent; designed for peer-to-peer growth among ~45 members.
3. **No Fluff**: Clean UI without unnecessary clutter, ads, or distraction.

---

## Visual Tone

- **Aesthetic**: Modern, clean, dark-mode elevated interface with vibrant sky-blue accents.
- **Typography**: Crisp sans-serif fonts prioritising legibility on all devices.
