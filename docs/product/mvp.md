# MVP Scope & Specifications

## Overview

The Minimum Viable Product (MVP) for **Forge** is intentionally small, focused on serving a private learning community of approximately **45 members**. 

The goal of the MVP is to provide a clean, reliable, and engaging web platform to organize learning activities, team challenges, resources, and progress tracking without unnecessary overhead.

---

## MVP Core Modules

1. **Learning Activities**: Catalog and status tracking of learning modules, tasks, and objectives.
2. **Collaborative Challenges**: Group tasks and cohort-wide goals designed for peer participation.
3. **Teams**: Creation, membership, and management of learning teams within the 45-member community.
4. **Resources**: Shared repository for curating and accessing learning links, guides, and materials.
5. **Leaderboards**: Visibility into individual and team activity points and progress rankings.
6. **Progress Tracking**: Personal and cohort-level visual metrics on completed activities and milestones.

---

## What is Excluded from MVP

- No AI components or automated tutors.
- No automated notifications or email delivery.
- No integrated messaging forums or chat channels.
- No complex analytics engines or data pipelines.
- No hackathon management workflows.
- No automated quiz engines or grading systems.
- No complex third-party API integrations.

---

## Success Criteria for MVP

- Members can log in securely and view active community activities and challenges.
- Members can join or view teams and access shared learning resources.
- Activity progress updates accurately update individual scores and leaderboard rankings.
- System operates cleanly and responsively for all ~45 community members.
