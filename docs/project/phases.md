# Project Execution Phases

## Overview

To keep development maintainable and ensure high software quality, Forge development is strictly structured into phases. Only features allocated to the active phase may be implemented.

---

## Phase 1: MVP Foundation (ACTIVE PHASE)

### Focus
Deliver a lightweight, robust, web-based platform tailored for ~45 community members.

### In-Scope Capabilities
- **Learning Activities**: Organization and display of community learning tasks/modules.
- **Collaborative Challenges**: Shared goals and group challenges.
- **Team Management**: Formation and view of community teams.
- **Resource Repository**: Access to community learning resources.
- **Leaderboards**: Visibility into community participation and score tracking.
- **Progress Tracking**: Individual and team progress metrics.

---

## Future Phases (OUT OF SCOPE FOR PHASE 1)

> [!WARNING]
> The following features are explicitly out of scope for Phase 1. They must **NOT** be implemented, stubbed, or integrated until Phase 1 is completely verified and delivered.

- **AI Features**: AI tutors, automated feedback, or generative content.
- **Notifications**: Email, push, or real-time notification servers.
- **Forums / Discussion Boards**: Community messaging feeds or threaded discussions.
- **Analytics**: Advanced data pipelines or metrics dashboards.
- **Hackathons**: Event management workflows.
- **Quizzes & Assessments**: Test creation or automated grading engines.
- **Integrations**: Third-party OAuth or external service integrations (beyond basic auth).

---

## Document Purpose & Beginner Context

### Why does this document exist?
It acts as a strict guardrail for product scope, dividing the project lifecycle into manageable stages.

### What problem does it solve?
It prevents premature optimization and feature creep, ensuring that time is spent perfecting the core community experience before adding advanced features.
