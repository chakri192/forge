# Project Vision - Forge

## Overview

- **Public Name**: Forge
- **Internal Codename**: Operation Overthink
- **Target Audience**: Private learning community (~45 members)

## Core Purpose

Forge is a web-based platform designed to serve as the **operating system for a private learning community**. It helps community members organize learning activities, engage in collaborative challenges, manage teams, access shared resources, track individual and group progress, and view leaderboards.

## What Forge Is NOT

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Forge is **NOT** intended to become a generic Learning Management System (LMS). Traditional LMS platforms focus on standardized course delivery, grading, and compliance tracking. Forge focuses on community engagement, peer collaboration, shared challenges, and collective growth.

## Core Value Proposition

1. **Community-Centric**: Built specifically around team collaboration, peer learning, and collective achievement.
2. **Intentional & Lean**: The initial version (MVP) remains small and tightly focused on core community interactions without feature bloat.
3. **Foundation for Growth**: Designed to support future capabilities (such as AI, analytics, or integrations) in later phases without overcomplicating the current architecture.

## Document Purpose & Beginner Context

### Why does this document exist?
This document establishes the high-level vision and boundaries of the Forge project. Every architectural, design, and product decision made during development must align with this core purpose.

### What problem does it solve?
It prevents "scope creep" (adding unnecessary features) and keeps the engineering team focused on creating a tailored community experience rather than a bloated, generic LMS.
